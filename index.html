The Konakai experience

Three decades of Peruvian cooking, one family.

Konakai has been a family restaurant since 1992. We cook the whole of Peru: lime-cured ceviches from the coast, Creole classics like lomo saltado and ají de gallina, and the Chifa, the Peruvian-Chinese wok cooking that made us famous.

Fresh fish, real Peruvian ají and recipes passed down at home. A warm, family room made for birthdays, Sunday lunches and long dinners with friends.

On Friday and Saturday nights the party keeps going with karaoke from 9 PM. Birthdays, special events and every kind of celebration have a place at Konakai.

  • Chifa specialistsFried rice and wok-fired saltados with real smoky flavor.
  • From the seaCeviches, parihuela, chalaca mussels and octopus anticuchero.
  • Flavors of the AmazonTacacho, cecina and juane, hard to find anywhere else in Florida.
